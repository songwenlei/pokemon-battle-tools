# Pokemon Team Building Checklist

Use this checklist before testing a team.

## Type Coverage

- Does the team have answers to common offensive types?
- Are there repeated defensive weaknesses?
- Does the team rely on only one Pokemon to cover a major threat?

Tool:

https://globalplay.games/tools/pokemon-team-builder

## Role Balance

- Physical attacker
- Special attacker
- Defensive pivot
- Speed control
- Setup control
- Hazard pressure or removal

## Testing Notes

After checking weaknesses, test the team in real battles and update the structure based on repeated losses.
