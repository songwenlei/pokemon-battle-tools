# Nuzlocke Boss Fight Planning

Use case: gym leaders, rivals, elite four fights, champions, and romhack boss battles.

## Planning Checklist

1. Write down the boss team, levels, abilities, and known items.
2. Build your available team around guaranteed switch-ins first.
3. Check type weaknesses and avoid stacking the same weakness.
4. Calculate the key damage ranges before entering the fight.
5. Plan safe pivots and emergency routes.
6. Avoid relying on a low-probability damage roll unless there is no safer line.

Tools:

- https://globalplay.games/tools/pokemon-team-builder
- https://globalplay.games/tools/pokemon-damage-calculator

## Damage Questions To Check

- Does the lead survive the first hit?
- Does the planned move KO after chip damage?
- Does a held item change the survival range?
- Can the backup Pokemon enter safely if the first plan fails?
- Is there a safer two-turn line than a risky one-turn roll?
