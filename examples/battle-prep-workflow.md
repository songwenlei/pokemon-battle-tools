# Pokemon Battle Preparation Workflow

Use case: tournament sets, ladder testing, friendly battles, draft league prep, and important Nuzlocke fights.

## Preparation Steps

1. List the expected threats.
2. Check whether the team has repeated defensive weaknesses.
3. Confirm speed control options.
4. Run damage calculations for the most important attacker and defender pairs.
5. Record reliable KOs, survival ranges, and risky rolls.
6. Adjust items, moves, or roles only when the calculation changes a real decision.

Tools:

- https://globalplay.games/tools/pokemon-team-builder
- https://globalplay.games/tools/pokemon-damage-calculator

## Notes Template

| Matchup | Question | Result | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attacker vs defender | Does the move KO? | Record the range | Keep or adjust |
| Defender vs threat | Can it survive? | Record the range | Switch, scout, or avoid |
| Setup sweeper | Is boost required? | Record threshold | Plan setup turn |
